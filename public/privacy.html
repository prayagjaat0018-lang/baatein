Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

In short: Baatein is a chat app made by Prayag (Udaipur, India). We collect only what the app needs to work. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads. Your messages are not end-to-end encrypted (see section 5).

1. What we collect

  • Account: email address and password (the password is stored only as a secure hash by our login provider).
  • Profile: name, username, bio, profile photo, and your privacy settings.
  • Content you create: chat messages, photos, voice notes, statuses (with optional song details), posts, reels, comments, likes, reactions.
  • Social data: who you follow, who follows you, follow requests, blocked users, and reports you submit.
  • Activity data: call history (who called whom, when, and for how long; call audio/video itself is not recorded), reward points, badges and invite/referral records.
  • Notification data: if you turn on notifications, your browser/phone push subscription details.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider may log basic technical information such as IP address and device type for security and troubleshooting.

2. How we use it

  • To run the app: deliver messages, show profiles, posts and statuses, make calls, send notifications.
  • To keep it safe: handle blocks and reports, prevent abuse of the points system.
  • To improve the app and fix problems.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Who can see what

  • Chat messages are visible to the people in that chat. Group messages are visible to group members.
  • Your name, username, photo and bio are visible to other users, subject to your privacy settings (for example, you can limit who sees your photo and bio, hide your online status, or make your account private).
  • Posts, reels and statuses are visible according to the audience rules in the app (public accounts, followers, or people you chat with).
  • Reports you submit are seen only by the app owner.

4. Service providers

Baatein uses these providers to work. They process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase – login, database and file storage.
  • Render – hosting of the app server.
  • Apple iTunes Search – used to search 30-second song previews for statuses. Your search words are sent to it.
  • YouTube / Instagram – if a reel is shared as a link, it is shown using their embed player and their own terms and privacy policies apply to that content.
  • Browser/phone push services (for example Google or Apple) – to deliver notifications.

5. Security and encryption (please read)

  • All data travels over HTTPS/secure connections, and our storage provider encrypts stored data.
  • Access rules in the database allow only chat members to read a chat.
  • Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. This means the app owner, who administers the database, could technically access stored messages. We commit not to read private chats except where required to investigate a report, to enforce our Terms, or to comply with the law.
  • Chat Lock on your device is a convenience feature that hides chats inside the app. It is not encryption.
  • No system is perfectly secure, so please do not share highly sensitive information such as bank passwords.

6. How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account exists. Statuses expire automatically after 24 hours. You can delete individual messages, posts and comments. If you delete your account (Profile → Account → Delete), your profile and related account data are deleted from our database. Copies in backups, and files in storage, may remain for a short time before they are removed.

7. Your choices

  • Edit your profile and privacy settings anytime (Profile → Privacy).
  • Block or report users, and unblock them later.
  • Turn notifications off in your browser or phone settings.
  • Delete your account from inside the app, or ask us to by email.

8. Children

Baatein is not meant for children under 13. If you are under 18, please use the app only with the permission of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Important changes will be announced in the app.
